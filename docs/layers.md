# Two layers: How Centaurs Work and the POM harness

This repo holds two plugins.

- **How Centaurs Work (HCW)** is the **core**. It's for an individual person working on their own goals and the relational work of their job, with no institutional coordination needed.
- **The POM harness** is a **layer on top**. It's institutional in scope: for a workshop cohort at minimum, moving an organisation toward a product operating model. It always has a named sponsor who owns a goal.

"Harness" here means the methodology sense, as in Superpowers: the skills and bootstrap instructions that make an agent work in a disciplined way. It doesn't mean the agent runtime.

## The seam

**HCW provides the mechanisms. The POM layer provides institutional content and rules.**

| Lives in HCW (the core) | Lives in the POM layer |
|---|---|
| The three principles of HCW, and the emotion corollary | The target (H2+) and the intent (H3) |
| The centaur test, questions 1–7 | Question 8: is this H2+ or H2−? |
| The spine and its skills (see what good looks like → name your delta → state your goal → practise → act → report back → notice progress → recommit, raise the bar, or teach) | The sponsor's goal, and each person's own link to it |
| The *mechanism* for seeing the delta: human-written examples shown verbatim; the person names the gap | The *content*: product-operating-model examples, including what effective executive leadership looks like |
| The coach and the progression ladder (practising → proficient → expert → teaching) | Cohort delivery, and teaching inside the organisation |
| The four pillars (relationships, influence, clarity, strategy) as individual practices | The pillars pointed at the product operating model, plus executive habits |
| Each person owns their goals, logs and records | No reporting up across a shared deployment |

## Rules

1. **Refer by name, never fork.** A POM skill refers to core skills by name. It never restates or copies them. If a POM skill needs something the core lacks, the core gets it.
2. **The layer degrades to the core.** No sponsor, no clear executive leadership, or no cohort means no POM layer. The person keeps practising toward their own goals with HCW.
3. **Same principles in both layers.** "Institutional" never means top-down. The institution supplies a destination and examples, never anyone's goals.
4. **Use stubs, not forks.** While a core skill doesn't exist yet, a POM skill records the dependency and keeps going. It never writes its own version.
5. **Check for drift now and then.** Does any POM skill restate a principle or fork a core skill? Fix it in the core.

## The three principles of HCW

```
1. NEVER SET, INFER, OR SUBSTITUTE A GOAL.
   Goals are stored as the human said them. Ask; do not rephrase.

2. NEVER SUPPLY CLARITY.
   Ask the question. The human answers it. Clarity always comes from humans.

3. DYSFUNCTION IS NOT A PERSONAL FAILING, BY DEFAULT.
   Always assume first that people want to do their best, and are doing their
   best in the context they are in. Assume they have not had the training,
   skills or experience. Assume their environment is at worst hostile, at best
   indifferent. Assume management does not know how to support them, even
   when it agrees and is aligned.

   Humans are emotional creatures. People often act out of fear,
   and the fear can be justified.
```

Every skill is written the Superpowers way (`writing-skills`): no skill without a failing test first. Test material is kept in a separate private repository, so agents can't learn the answers.
